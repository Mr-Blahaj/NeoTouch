--- slide 5 ---
TOUCHWALL — CV ENGINE
Hand Tracker v3 — rebuilt from scratch for production reliability.
v3
OneEuroFilter1D smoothing
Per-landmark (21 × {x,y,z}) filters eliminate jitter at the source.
v3
Velocity-gated pinch
Accidental triggers while moving are blocked. Real pinch occurs nearly stationary.
v3
Temporal pinch hysteresis
Confirmed across multiple consecutive frames. Eliminates single-frame glitch triggers.
v3
Full hand-plane depth (Z)
Uses entire hand plane — not a single fingertip — for accurate push-forward depth.
v3
distanceFromScreen scaling
Scales depth thresholds for 50cm–3m from wall. Same feel at any projection range.
v3
Frame-rate-independent
Real time deltas (dt tracking): stable frequency response at 30fps, 60fps, or variable.

--- slide 8 ---
ADAPTIVE SCORING
Designed to keep children in the zone — less boredom, less frustration.
P(θ) = c + (1 − c) / (1 + e^{−a(θ−b)})
3PL-IRT — Three Parameter Logistic Item Response Theory
θ
Student ability
Updated Bayesian after each attempt. Personalised per child.
b
Item difficulty
Set by prototype parameters. Pilot calibration planned.
a
Item discrimination
How sharply the item separates adjacent ability levels.
c
Guessing parameter
Chance-level floor. Prevents lucky streaks from inflating θ.
